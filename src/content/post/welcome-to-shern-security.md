## Hello and Welcome

I'm excited to launch Shern Security - a unique blend of professional cybersecurity consulting and innovative technology solutions for families.

## Who Am I?

I'm a cybersecurity professional with years of experience in network security, penetration testing, and secure systems architecture. Throughout my career, I've helped organizations protect their digital assets and build robust security practices.

But I'm also a parent, and I've experienced firsthand the challenges of managing technology in family life.

## The Mission

Shern Security has a dual mission:

### 1. Professional Security Services

Providing expert cybersecurity consulting to organizations that need:
- Security audits and vulnerability assessments
- Penetration testing and threat modeling
- Secure architecture design
- Incident response and remediation
- Security training and awareness programs

### 2. Innovative Family Technology

Building tools that help families navigate the digital world responsibly. My first project in this space is **Access Granted** - a parental control solution that teaches responsibility rather than just imposing restrictions.

## Why This Blog?

This blog serves several purposes:

- **Share security insights** - Tips, best practices, and analysis of emerging threats
- **Document development** - Progress updates on Access Granted and other projects
- **Build community** - Connect with other security professionals and parents facing similar challenges
- **Educate** - Make security concepts accessible to everyone

## What's Coming

Over the coming weeks and months, you can expect posts on:

- Security best practices for small businesses
- Deep dives into specific security tools and techniques
- Updates on Access Granted development
- Thoughts on responsible technology use
- Real-world security case studies
